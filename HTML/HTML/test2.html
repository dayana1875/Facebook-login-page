Mahatma Gandhi

Mahatma Gandhi was a great leader who played an important role in India’s freedom struggle. He was born on October 2, 1869, in Porbandar, Gujarat. Gandhi followed the principles of truth and non-violence and inspired millions of people. He led movements like the Non-Cooperation Movement and the Quit India Movement against British rule. People lovingly called him the “Father of the Nation.” His simple life, honesty, and dedication continue to inspire people all over the world.

Subhas Chandra Bose was a brave freedom fighter and nationalist leader who played a major role in India’s struggle for independence. He was born on January 23, 1897, in Cuttack, Odisha. Bose strongly believed that freedom could be achieved through courage and action. He formed the Indian National Army (INA) to fight against British rule and gave the famous slogan, “Give me blood, and I will give you freedom.” He is popularly known as “Netaji” and is remembered for his patriotism, leadership, and sacrifice for the nation.
